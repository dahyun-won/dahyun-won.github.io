Research Core

The puzzle I want to solve is by what mechanisms an emotion becomes pathological. What makes this puzzle compelling is that it has two faces at once: a set of symptom patterns shared across a clinical population, and individual differences that this shared pattern alone cannot explain. Thus, my work aims to disentangle the shared and idiosyncratic neurophysiological mechanisms underlying affective disorders and related conditions, focusing on the dynamic interplay among emotion, cognition, and body.

To pursue these questions, I am currently involved in the projects below.

  • Intrinsic connectivity of individual differences in anxiety sensitivity
  • Common and distinct mechanisms of anxiety and depression
  • Universality and cultural specificity in misophonia: A cross-national study
  • Functional connectivity patterns underlying children's curiosity
  • Affective disorders
  • Neurophysiological mechanisms
  • Brain–body interaction
  • Emotion–cognition interaction
  • Individual differences
